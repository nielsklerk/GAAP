\chapter{Abstract}

Accurate photometry is important for different applications, such as source classification and photometric redshift. However, the effect of the PSF and seeing on observation results in systematic errors in the photometric colors of sources. This work develops cross-band aperture photometry: X-BAP, a new method that applies a PSF-corrected aperture to the intrinsic image, before the effects of the PSF and seeing, by constructing a post-seeing aperture based on the PSF from a pre-seeing aperture that is applied to all bands. The method is validated and verified on simulated observations before being applied to Q1 MER mosaics of the Euclid mission for source classification. These results show that X-BAP gives accurate results in the case of the simulations and prove to be useful for large surveys such as Euclid for color measurements.
